Removal Method
🎨Smart Fill
Analyzes the pixels along all 4 edges of the selected area, then fills the watermark region by blending those surrounding colors inward using weighted interpolation. The result looks like the background was never covered — the most natural-looking removal method.
⚙ Samples edge pixels → bilinear blend → seamless fill
🌫️Blur
Applies a heavy Gaussian blur to the selected region every frame. The watermark becomes visually unreadable — text and logos dissolve into a smeared haze. Works well when the watermark sits on a complex or colorful background.
⚙ Downscale → upscale trick + CSS filter blur, multi-pass
⬛Black Box
Paints a solid black (#000000) rectangle over the watermark area on every frame. Simple, fast, and 100% effective at hiding the watermark. Best choice when the video has a dark background or when a clean opaque cover is acceptable.
⚙ fillRect with #000000 per frame — fastest method
🔲Pixelate
Shrinks the selected region down to a tiny grid of large pixels and scales it back up, creating a mosaic/censor-bar effect. The watermark becomes completely unreadable while the blocked area still reflects the original colors. Intensity slider controls block size.
⚙ Downscale to N×M → upscale with imageSmoothingEnabled: false
